# Design System: ghoops

Extracted on 2026-11-12 from the shipped SwiftUI code and updated on 2026-11-13 after the design cleanup. **The code is the authority:** `ios/Shared/Goop/GoopTokens.swift`, `GoopFont.swift`, `ios/GHealth/Components/Goop/*` and the screens. The approved references are `docs/goop/spec.md` and the mockups `goop.html`, `activity.html` and `ghoops-wordmark.html`. The current `edit.html` and its eight screen renders were approved on 2026-11-12; [edit-spec.md](docs/goop/edit-spec.md) defines their behaviour.

Frontmatter sizes are SwiftUI points written as `px` for tool compatibility, so 1px means 1pt. Frontmatter keys use the Swift token names, so `colors.strain` is `Goop.Color.strain` and `typography.labelCard` is `Goop.TextStyle.labelCard`. Wherever the frontmatter lists one colour for a surface, the code draws a vertical `…Top → …Bottom` gradient; see Elevation & Depth.

Items marked **(provisional)** are creative language or proposals the owner has not confirmed. Everything else is observed in code or written in an approved spec.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Quiet Scoreboard"**

ghoops is a scoreboard you read half-awake. The numbers are the loudest thing on every screen: big condensed Sofia numerals inside rings and at the right edge of rows. Everything around them is quiet, dark blue-grey slate that recedes. Colour carries meaning rather than decoration. Every hue belongs to a score (sleep blue-grey, recovery traffic light, strain blue), to a heart-rate zone or sleep stage, or to a status (mint, amber). Chrome stays white, grey and slate.

The density is WHOOP-like: stacked slabs with 12–14pt corners, uppercase tracked labels at 12–13pt, one column inside a 16pt gutter, and no ornament. Depth comes only from tone, a few steps of slate gradient on a fixed background gradient, never from shadows. Interaction is plain. Whole rows and cards are the buttons. Headers are rows that scroll away. A screen holds at most one filled action button.

The visual world is the approved mockup set in `docs/goop/mockups/`. The current `edit.html` and its eight screen renders were approved on 2026-11-12. New features extend the existing slabs, rows, banners and sheets. Capsule pills, text nav buttons, steppers, switches, toasts and side-by-side buttons remain outside this system. The one pair that shares a row is the activities card's ADD ACTIVITY and START ACTIVITY (The Entry-Pair Rule).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Always-dark slate world: fixed vertical background gradients from `#293339` down to `#0E1313`.
- Two typefaces: Sofia Sans Condensed 750 for every measured number and Nunito Sans for words.
- Labels are uppercase and tracked 0.10–0.14em; sentences are sentence case.
- Colour is data: score, zone, stage and status hues only.
- Flat tonal slabs with no shadows, corner radii from 8 to 14, and capsules used only for the date control.
- Whole-surface tap targets, a custom tab bar, and headers that scroll away with the content.
- Honest gaps: `—` in grey, stale values at 55% opacity.

## Colors

A cool slate neutral world in which saturated colour appears only when it stands for a score, a zone, a stage or a status. Display names are provisional.

### Primary
- **Strain Blue** (`strain`): Strain's ring, bars and value. It is also the **only interactive accent**, used for selection outlines, the commit button of a task sheet, live-activity outlines and the text-field cursor. It may act as the accent only because activities are strain. `strainLight` and `strainPale` are its light tints; `strainDeep` is its dark tint.
- **Signal Blue** (`workoutLine`): the line of the workout heart-rate chart. Its area fill is `strain` at 35% → 0%.

### Secondary
- **Dawn Slate** (`sleep`): the Sleep ring and sleep activity blocks. Naps use it at 60%.
- **Recovery Green / Amber / Red** (`recoveryHigh`, `recoveryMedium`, `recoveryLow`): the recovery band, used only on the Recovery ring, recovery bars and the weekly recovery chart. These are band colours, not UI semantics.

### Tertiary
- **Mint** (`positive`, `healthOK`): within range, an improvement, a live heart-rate connection.
- **Amber** (`caution`): attention, staleness and errors. It is the symbol colour on banners, the WAITING FOR MAC chip, `RECONNECTING` on live surfaces, and error text.
- **Insight Violet** (`insightAction`, with the `insightBorderStart` → `insightBorderEnd` border): used only on the insight card's action link and border.
- **Data lines** (`trendLine`, `hrLine`, `hrFill`, `dayHR`): chart strokes and fills.

### Data scales
- **Zones 0–5** (`zone0` … `zone5`): from near-white through blue, teal and orange to `#FD7138`. They appear on zone rows, the live zone meter and the live HR chart (`zone4`). `zone5` is also the end and delete colour (see Components).
- **Sleep stages** (`stageAwake`, `stageLight`, `stageDeep`, `stageREM`): used in the hypnogram, stage rows and the restorative swatch. `stageUnstaged` marks time inside a night that has no stage: the hypnogram's unstaged block and the `UNSTAGED` row (The Unstaged Rule).

### Neutral
- **Text ramp:** `text1` (white; values, titles), `bodyText` (Home wordmark, trend sentence), `legendText` (banner and legend text, sheet body), `textSoft` (secondary values, subtitles, disclosure chevrons), `text2` (captions, placeholders, `—`), `iconMuted` (icons, section titles in More, footnotes), `chevronDial`, and `text3` (inactive tabs and input placeholders only).
- **Backgrounds:** `GoopBackground` draws home (`#293339` at 0 → `#1A222B` 224 → `#13171C` 459 → `backgroundHomeBase` 740pt) and detail (`#273238` → `#1D252B` → `#14191D` → `backgroundDetailBase`). Both are fixed behind the scroll content.
- **Surfaces, light to dark:** `activityRow` > `tileTop`/`monitorTop` > `dateOuter` > `cardTop` > `controlFill`/`zoneRowFill`/`chipFill` > `activitiesFill` > `contributorFillTop` > `sheetFill`/`tabTop`. Reserve `legendFill` for the nap-credit mark inside the need bar (The Lighter-Inside Rule).
- **Lines:** `ringTrack`, `divider`, `cardDivider`, `contributorBorder`, `plotGrid`, `chartGrid`, `outlineBorder` (outline buttons).

### Named Rules
**The Score Owns Its Colour Rule.** Recovery green/amber/red, Sleep slate, the zones and the stages mean data and nothing else. A button, badge or banner never borrows them for emphasis. In particular, red means low recovery, never "delete".

**The Token-Only Rule.** Every colour on screen comes from `Goop.Color` or `Goop.Gradient`. A `Color(goopHex:)` literal outside `GoopTokens.swift` is drift. Add a named token first and use it second.

**The Missing-Is-Grey Rule.** A null value renders as `—` in `text2` and never as `0`. Values older than 6 hours render at `Goop.Opacity.stale` (55%) through `AppModel.valuesOpacity`. Grey and dimming are the only signs of absence or staleness; colour is never removed from a value for any other reason. A widget's age counts from the health data (the Mac's last collection or the band's last sync, whichever is older), never from the download, so a fresh download of old data never says `now`. When a widget's refresh fails after a possible wake, its scores carry their day (`Tue 10`), or `—` where there is no room (issue #32).

**The Amber Is A Pattern Rule.** On the Health Monitor card, the vital tiles and the HRV widget, amber appears only when the Mac reports `monitor_state: attention`, and only on the vitals it lists in `persistent`. A vital outside its range on one night shows the grey triangle, whichever way it moved. The phone never decides amber from `adverse`.

**The Unstaged Rule.** Time inside a night that no stage covers is drawn as one `stageUnstaged` block from the REM lane to the Deep lane and listed as an `UNSTAGED` row. It is never drawn in a stage's colour, never left as empty space, and never filled with a guessed stage.

**The No-Borrowed-Stage Rule.** Only Google's `awake`, `light`, `deep` and `rem` are drawn in the stage lanes. Google's classic `asleep` and `restless`, and any stage value the app doesn't know, are never drawn as a stage or spoken as one. On the Sleep screen a classic night shows "Classic sleep: no sleep stages for this night" in place of the hypnogram and keeps its duration. An unknown value inside a staged night is left out of the lanes and spoken as "Unknown stage" (issue #32).

## Typography

**Display Font:** Sofia Sans Condensed at a fixed `wght` of 750 (`GoopFont.sofia`)
**Body Font:** Nunito Sans variable (`GoopFont.nunito`), weights 500–750, opsz 12, YTLC 500
**Label/Mono Font:** none. Labels are Nunito Sans uppercase.

**Character:** Condensed numerals give the scoreboard punch while staying narrow enough for right-aligned columns. Nunito's rounded humanist forms soften the uppercase labels so the dense screens never feel clinical. *(provisional)*

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (`scoreDetail` 76, `scoreHome` 30; Sofia 750; line height 1): the score inside a ring. The `%` is Sofia at 62% of the value size. Fixed size: Dynamic Type does not scale it.
- **Values** (`valueAverage` 38 → `valueStat` 34 → `valueVital` 31 → `valueHours` 28 → `valueRow` 23 → `valueZone` 20 → `valueStage` 18 → `valueMid` 17 → `valueSmall` 14 → `valueTiny` 13; Sofia 750; cap 1.35×): every measured value outside a ring. Comparison means sit beneath them in `compareRow` 11.5 or `compareTile` 11 in `text2`.
- **Headline** (`heading`, Nunito 650, 22, −0.01em): section headings in title case (My Day, My Dashboard, Weekly Trends, Overnight Vitals) and full-screen state titles. These are the only title-case strings.
- **Title** (`labelNav` 13 N700 0.14em; `labelWorkoutTitle` 15 N750 0.12em): screen and sheet titles, uppercase.
- **Body** (`body` 15/21.3, `bodyMid` 13.5/18.9, `bodySmall` 12.5/18.1; N550; cap 1.6×): insight sentences, banners, sheet prose, footnotes and captions. Sentence case.
- **Label** (`labelCard` 12.5 N750 0.10em is the workhorse; `labelSmall` 12, `labelStage` 13, `labelActivity` 13.5 N700 0.12em, `labelAction` 12.5 N700 0.12em, `labelDate` 12, `labelSegment` 12.5; cap 1.5×): every card title, row label, button label and stat caption. Uppercase strings are passed already uppercased, with a sentence-case `accessibilityLabel`.
- **Additional labels** (`labelTiny` 11, `labelMicro` 10, `labelStat` 10.5, `labelPicker` 13.5; N750 0.10em, cap 1.5×): compact status, stat and picker labels. `labelRing` 12.5 and `labelCommit` 14 use 0.14em. `labelTimerPill` 8 uses 0.10em. `labelLockRow` 11 uses 0.08em and cap 1 for the rectangular Lock Screen widget.
- **Captions and row text** (`captionDial` 10.5, `rowNote` 12, `chip` 11.5, `unitSmall` 10; N550, cap 1.6×): dial captions, choice notes, chips and units. `captionWidget` 10 stays fixed at cap 1; `unitLarge` 16 is N500 at cap 1.35×.
- **Fixed values** (`valueChip` 11.5, `valueBlock` 21, `valueLiveRow` 20, `valueTimer` 44, `scoreLive` 68, `valueLiveHR` 46, `valueZoneRange` 13, `valueLiveStat` 22, `valueLiveActivityHR` 40, `valueIsland` 18; Sofia 750, cap 1): values inside fixed frames in rows, the live screen and Live Activity. `valueTimer` has 0.02em tracking and monospaced digits. These follow the app's scaling below Large and stay fixed at Large and above; widgets and preview hosts force Large.
- **Chart text** (`axis` 11, `axisTime` 12 in Sofia; `axisDay` 12 / `axisDayNumber` 10.5 in Nunito; `labelLane` 10.5): fixed size, drawn in `Canvas` through `GoopPlot.text`.

### Named Rules
**The Two Voices Rule.** Every measured number (scores, bpm, minutes, strain, percentages, chart values) is Sofia Sans Condensed 750. Words are Nunito Sans. A number in Nunito or a word in Sofia is drift. Three cases need care:
- **A Sofia string is a number and nothing else.** It holds digits, spaces and the signs `. , : % + − – — <`, never a letter. Write a range as numbers: `0–69%`, not `under 70%`. A unit is its own Nunito text after the number on the same baseline, for example `unitSmall` after `valueMid` or a smaller value, `unit` after `valueVital` and `unitLarge` after `valueAverage`. Leave the unit out when the value is `—`. `GoopStatCells`, `GoopSheetRow` and the vital tiles show the pattern. A word that stands where a number would, such as "Building baseline" in a vital chip, takes that slot's Nunito style (`chip`).
- **A number inside words keeps the words' voice.** Labels, row notes, chip phrases and sentences are Nunito even when they hold a number: "ZONE 5", "100% of need", "56 ms · usual 52 · 55% weight", "7 nights to start". Never split them into Sofia and Nunito runs.
- **Status text is Nunito.** The band battery (`85%`) and "Synced 14:08" describe the device and the sync, not a measurement.
- **Dates, and clock times in rows and footers, are Nunito.** Weekday, day and month labels on charts use `axisDay`, `axisDayNumber` or Nunito Canvas text. Clock times in activity rows and footers use `time`. A clock time shown as a value, such as the ends of a `GoopWindowBar` or Tonight's wake, stays Sofia.
- **The pairing comparison code** in the pairing task uses Sofia `valueAverage` on a `controlFill` panel. It shows six digits in two groups so people can compare them at a glance.

The goop lint's `sofiaword` rule fails `make check` when a string literal with a letter reaches Sofia text.

**The Uppercase Label Rule.** Anything that names something is uppercase Nunito at 700–750 with 0.10–0.14em tracking. Anything that says something is sentence case at N550. Headings are the only title case. No other case style exists.

**The Enum-Only Rule.** Text gets its font only through `.goop(.style)`. `GoopTextStyle(size:…)` and `Font(GoopFont.sofia(n))` in a screen or component bypass the type scale and the Dynamic Type caps. If a size is missing, add a case to `Goop.TextStyle` with its cap, so the gallery shows it.

## Layout

- **One column inside a 16pt gutter** (`screenMargin`). Home section headings sit 4pt further in (`homeTitleInset`). Full-screen states and Pairing use 24.
- **Vertical rhythm:**
  - 8 between banners, sheet rows and zone rows;
  - 12 between stacked cards and between an activity row and the next;
  - 22 from the dials to the monitor cards;
  - 24 above a sheet section;
  - 26 above a notched contributor card;
  - 30 above a section heading or destructive button;
  - 30 of bottom spacer at the end of every detail page.
- **Internal padding:** cards 16 (charts 14 with 6 at the bottom); tiles 16 horizontal; activities card 16/13/13; rows 9–14 horizontal.
- **Two-up grids** (monitor cards, vital tiles) are built from `GoopPair` rows with 11–13 gaps. Both cards in a row take the taller card's height, and the row collapses to one column at accessibility sizes. Rows and tiles also stack the value under the label at those sizes.
- **Chrome scrolls with the content.** The system navigation bar and tab bar are hidden. `GoopDetailHeader` (50 tall) is the first row of the `ScrollView`, and `InteractivePopEnabler` keeps the edge swipe working. The custom `GoopTabBar` appears only at tab roots, and root scroll views reserve 84pt for it.
- **Tap targets:** at least 44×44 everywhere, with visible glyphs as small as 11pt inside invisible padding (`contentShape(Rectangle().inset(by: -14))`).
- **Portrait iPhone only**, laid out at 390–402pt wide.

### Named Rules
**The 16-Point Gutter Rule.** Content edges align at 16pt from the screen edge. Nothing bleeds to the edge except backgrounds, the tab bar, and charts that pad themselves inside a card.

**The One-Line Title Rule.** A card title or status label is one line, as WHOOP sets them. Never put a hard line break (`\n`) in a title, label or status. A title that might not fit gets `.lineLimit(1).minimumScaleFactor(0.75)`. A tile title in a narrow column may wrap naturally to two lines. The owner rejected forced two-line titles on the Home monitor cards on 2026-11-14.

**The Scroll-Away Chrome Rule.** Headers are content. Never bring back a system navigation bar, large titles or a toolbar on a ghoops screen, and never break the edge swipe back.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat and tonal. Depth is a lightness step between a fixed background gradient and the slabs on it, and each slab is itself a subtle top-to-bottom gradient (`Goop.Gradient.card`, `.tile`, `.monitor`, `.contributorFill`, `.insightFill`, `.tab`). Borders are rare and functional:
- 1pt `contributorBorder` around the notched card;
- 1.5pt `outlineBorder` on outline buttons;
- a 1.5pt gradient on the insight card;
- 1.5pt `strain` on the selected row and on the live row.

The only overlays are system sheets and the full-screen live cover.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Zone glow** (`shadow(color: zone.opacity(0.5), radius: 6)`): only on the current segment of the live zone meter (`GoopLiveActivityView.swift:13`). It is the one shadow in the system.

### Named Rules
**The Tone-Not-Shadow Rule.** Lift a surface by moving one step lighter on the surface ladder (see Colors → Neutral), never with a drop shadow, blur or glow. Floating elements such as toasts, FABs and popovers have no place here.

**The Lighter-Inside Rule.** A surface is never darker than the surface that holds it. A card holds no second box. A group of rows inside a card (a breakdown, a goal table, the band legend) sits straight on the card after a 1pt hairline, and its rows keep the card's own inset. The hairline is `cardDivider` in a `GoopCard` and `divider` in the notched card. On a sheet, a calculation or a code sits on one `controlFill` panel with radius 8, the same slab that inputs and list rows use. On the page, a neutral banner is a `tile` slab. The owner called the old near-black insets "black bg boxes" on 2026-11-24.

## Shapes

Gently rounded rectangles in a short ladder, picked by the element's role:
- **3** (`bar`): data bars and hatched bars.
- **5–6** (`badge`, `chip`): monitor badges, vital chips, comparison chips.
- **8** (`activityBlock`, `legend`, `rangeSegment`): blocks nested inside a row or strip.
- **10** (`picker`, `range`): controls and inputs (More rows, the metric picker, the search field, the range selector track, banners).
- **12** (`tile`, `insight`, `zoneRow`, `button`): tiles, zone rows, insight cards, every button, selected list rows, tinted banners.
- **13** (`monitor`, `activities`): monitor cards, the activities card and its rows.
- **14** (`card`): cards, including the notched contributor card.
- **Sheets:** system shape. `GoopTaskSheet` sets `radiusSheet` (26). Small marks use `radiusHairline` (1), `radiusMark` (2), `radiusSegment` (4) and `radiusHighlight` (5). `radiusBanner` is 10; `radiusBannerTinted` and `radiusChoice` are 12; `radiusLiveActivity` is 24.

The **notch** (an upward triangle with an 18pt base and 9pt height, centred on the top edge of the contributor card, pointing at the dial above) is the system's one signature silhouette. **Capsules** appear only in the date control and the Live Activity timer pill. **Circles** are rings, selection discs (stage rows) and dots.

**Dashes mean something:**
- a 1.5pt `[4,3]` `strain` border means detected effort;
- `typicalEdge` `[3,2]` edges mean the typical range;
- `naCredit` `[3,2]` means nap credit;
- white `[3,3]` or `[2.5,3]` verticals with baseline dots mean the start and end of a recorded window.

### Named Rules
**The Radius Ladder Rule.** Choose the radius from the role above, never by eye. Every button is 12, `Goop.Metric.radiusButton`. A new element that fits no rung is probably a new component, and it needs a token before it ships.

**The Dashes-Mean-Something Rule.** A dashed stroke always encodes one of the four meanings above. Never use a dash for decoration, for a "proposed" state, or as a second meaning on the same screen.

### Live Activity with a target

The bottom row has three equal cells: STRAIN, EST. DAY STRAIN and TARGET, separated by 1×23pt `plotGrid` rules. Its 22pt values replace the existing wordmark and AVG HR row without adding height: iOS may truncate a Live Activity above 160pt. The mint checkmark still means within range. See [parity1-spec §2.3](docs/goop/parity1-spec.md#23-live-activity-lock-live-haptic-and-alert).

### Live heart rate

Heart rate, zone and pause are separate facts on every live surface: the Live Activity, the Dynamic Island, the live screen and the Home live row. `LiveReadout` in GHealthCore decides the number, unit, meter zone and words. Views only map them to tokens.
- **Live, zone known:** the bpm in `text1` after a `zone5` heart; the meter lit to the zone; `ZONE 4` in the zone's colour.
- **Live, zone unknown** (the Mac has not sent resting or maximum heart rate, or `score_constants`): the bpm as above; the meter's empty track; `ZONE —` in `text2`.
- **No heart rate yet:** a `text2` heart and `—` in `text2` with no unit; the empty track; `NO HR` in `text2`.
- **Lost** (no reading for 10 s, or iOS marks the Live Activity stale): the last bpm, its heart and unit, and the meter at `Goop.Opacity.stale`; `RECONNECTING` in `caution`. Values the phone computed keep full strength.
- **Paused** changes only the timer and the dial caption.

**The Live Reading Rule.** `NO HR` means only that no heart rate has arrived. A missing zone or Strain never borrows it. Views never write `NO HR` or `RECONNECTING` themselves; the goop lint's `hrword` rule fails `make check` when they do.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** gently rounded (12, `rounded.button`) and full width. Each button sits alone on its row, except the activities card's entry pair (The Entry-Pair Rule).
- **Commit** (the primary action of a task): filled `strain`, 54 tall, a symbol plus an uppercase label, white. It uses `labelCommit` and is pinned to the bottom of `GoopTaskSheet` through `GoopCommit`. Use one per screen at most.
- **Outline (secondary):** `GoopButton(role: .outline)`, transparent with a 1.5pt `outlineBorder` stroke, 50 tall, `labelCard` white. Examples are VIEW TRENDS, TRY AGAIN, BACK TO TODAY, EDIT SLEEP under the Sleep screen's night card, and RESTORE GOOGLE'S VERSION. `GoopButton(role:)` has three roles: `.outline`, `.primary` and `.destructive`.
- **Light primary:** `GoopButton(role: .primary)`, a white fill with `backgroundHomeBase` text. It is used only for PAIR, the onboarding commit outside the strain world. Disabled is 40% opacity, and busy shows a `ProgressView` inside.
- **In-card action:** an `activityRow` fill, 40 tall, symbol plus `labelCard`, inside the activities card. On today's card, ADD ACTIVITY (`plus`) and START ACTIVITY (`stopwatch`) share a row as the entry pair. During a live session, END ‹SPORT› replaces the pair at full width and adds a 1pt `zone5` stroke at 33%. On a past day that ADD reaches, ADD ACTIVITY sits alone at full width.
- **Text action:** `labelAction` in `insightAction` plus `arrow.right` 13pt, used only as the insight card's link to its info sheet.
- **Hold control:** `HoldToEnd` is a long-press fill in `zone5` at 20% over 1.2s with a success haptic. It is reserved for ending a live session.
- **Pressed / focus:** plain button style with no highlight colour and no hover. Feedback comes from navigation or a haptic.

**The Entry-Pair Rule.** Two buttons share a row only as the activities card's ADD ACTIVITY and START ACTIVITY. They are two in-card actions of equal weight, neither filled, that open the two ways to log an activity. They stack, ADD first, whenever either label would not fit on one line, and END ‹SPORT› replaces them at full width while a session runs. Every other pair of actions stacks.

### Chips
- **Style:** `GoopChip` and the vital chip use 6 radius, `chipFill` (or the status hue at 12–15%) and 11.5pt text, with an optional comparison triangle.
- **State:** they are display-only and never tappable. A chip is not a button and not a filter.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 12–14 according to the Radius Ladder.
- **Background:** the `card`, `tile`, `monitor`, `contributorFill` or `insightFill` gradient. Flat fills are used for `activitiesFill`, `activityRow`, `zoneRowFill` and `controlFill`. A card holds no inner box (The Lighter-Inside Rule).
- **Shadow Strategy:** none (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** none, except the notched card (1pt) and the insight card (1.5pt gradient).
- **Internal Padding:** 16 standard, 13–14 for dense cards.
- **Interaction:** tappable cards and rows are a single `Button` with `.plain` style and a `chevron.right` (12pt, `textSoft`) at the right end of the title line, vertically centred on it, when they push. Rows never contain a second button.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** a `controlFill` slab with radius 10. The search field is 40 tall with `magnifyingglass` 15pt `chevronDial`, `bodyMid` text and the cursor tinted `strain`. The pairing code field is a multi-line variant with a 50pt minimum height and `radiusPicker` (10).
- **Focus:** the system cursor only. There is no glow and no border change.
- **Error / Disabled:** error text below the field in `bodyMid` `caution`. Disabled controls are at 40% opacity.

### Navigation
- **Detail header** (`GoopDetailHeader`): 50 tall, `chevron.left` 21pt in a 44×44 target on the left, centred `labelNav` title, and an optional `info.circle` 25pt `iconMuted` on the right. The right slot holds **one icon button or nothing**. `GoopWorkoutHeader` is the variant with a 30pt sport symbol, a title and a subtitle. Both headers accept one `GoopHeaderButton` in their `trailing:` slot.

The TODAY header, activities title, START/END action, live row and PAST day choices use that day. A live session keeps the `default_date` from its start, including when it starts after midnight. An added activity keeps its draft's day, captured when the sheet opens or chosen in the sheet. Saved rows and workout links use that stored day through relaunches. The phone never derives a ghoops day from a start time or copies the Mac's cycle rule; only older saved records without a day retain their calendar fallback.

- **Icons:** SF Symbols only, light weight by default, monochrome, through `GoopSymbol`. Each metric has its own symbol in `TrendMetricKey.icon`, which a Core test enforces. ### Sheets and modals
Two sheet types exist. A third presentation, the full-screen cover, is reserved for a session in progress.

- **Info sheet: `GoopSheet`.** It explains or lists, and the user commits nothing.
  - Large detent, visible grabber, flat `sheetFill`.
  - Header row 50 tall: a centred `labelNav` title and an `xmark` 13pt `iconMuted` close button on the **right** (`sheet-close`).
  - Content is padded 16. Sections are `GoopSheetSection` (an uppercase `labelCard` title 24 above, `bodyMid` `legendText` body). A section title may carry one trailing control, such as the Sleep sheet's 100% / 85% / 70% `GoopRangeSelector`. Its options are numbers, so it passes `numeric: true` and sets them in `valueSmall`. Key–value rows are `GoopSheetRow`: a `bodyMid` label with an optional `rowNote` note under it in `text2`, and a white Sofia 17 value right-aligned, with an optional `unitSmall` `text2` unit after it. They are not tappable, so they sit 7pt above and below rather than at 44pt.
  - Examples: the Recovery, Strain and Sleep info sheets and the full activities list. The approved explanation sheets are `docs/goop/mockups/sheets.html` (2026-11-16).
- **Task sheet: `GoopTaskSheet`.** The user makes choices, then commits.
  - Large detent, visible grabber, `radiusSheet` (26), and `Goop.Gradient.sheet` (`sheetTop → sheetMid → sheetBottom`).
  - Header: 50 tall, the same as an info sheet: a centred `labelNav` title and an `xmark` 13pt `iconMuted` close button on the **right**, which cancels and discards. A sub-step adds `chevron.left` on the left while keeping × on the right. Every sheet closes on the right, as the owner decided on 2026-11-12.
  - Content: optional status banner, then search, then uppercase section labels, then selection rows.
  - A **commit button pinned to the bottom** in a safe-area inset, with a label that names the result (START BOXING).
  - Swipe-down dismiss acts like the close button.
- **Full-screen cover: `LiveActivityScreen`.** Only for a running session. `chevron.down` on the left minimises it, the title is centred, and a status chip sits on the right.
- **Never:** text buttons in a header (Cancel, Save, Back, Done), a nested navigation stack inside a sheet, or a sheet drawn to fill the screen without its grabber.

**The Numbers-In-Rows Rule.** An explanation sheet shows its numbers in rows, breakdowns and scales, never inside sentences. The owner called the sentence version of the Sleep sheet "a wall of text" on 2026-11-16, and the Recovery and Strain sheets had the same problem.
- **A calculation** uses `GoopBreakdown`, a `controlFill` panel on the sheet with radius 8, after WHOOP's "how optimal sleep is calculated". Inside a card the rows sit on the card (The Lighter-Inside Rule). The panel holds the parts as `GoopBreakdownRow`s with a `legend` label, an optional `rowNote` note and a Sofia 14 value. Use a `GoopSwatch` when a bar above shows the parts. A hairline divides groups and a 1.5pt `bodyText` rule sits above the result. The result is a `GoopBreakdownTotal` in `labelCard` with a `valueHours` value. A subtotal at the top uses `valueRow`. The shown parts add up to the shown total.
- **A span between two clock times** is a `GoopWindowBar`: the times in `valueZone` above each end with a `labelTiny` caption, and a block between them in the score's colour that names the length. Its end marks are solid 2pt white lines, not dashes.
- **Score bands** are a `GoopLevelScale`: equal 4pt segments in the band colours with their ranges under them in `valueTiny`, written as numbers only (`0–69%`, `70–84%`, `85–100%`). The band that today's value is in is lit and white; the others sit at `Goop.Opacity.otherBand` with `text2` ranges. `GoopLevelHeader` names the bands once above a group of scales. This dimming marks position on a scale; it never stands for a missing or stale value.
- **Two to four related values**, such as breathing rate by stage, sit side by side in `GoopStatCells`, split by 1×23 `plotGrid` rules as on the Live Activity, and stack as rows at accessibility sizes.
- **A detail of a row**, such as the disturbance split or where a value comes from, is that row's note, never a line of its own.
- **Sentences** are left for the rule and the source: one `GoopFootnote` (`bodySmall`, `iconMuted`) of one or two short sentences per section. Metadata such as the score version is `GoopFinePrint`.
- **Task sub-steps:** `GoopTaskSheet(title:back:closeIdentifier:commit:content:)` takes the caller's content and an optional `GoopCommit`. A new task uses this component. A sub-choice replaces its title, content and commit from caller state, with `chevron.left` 21pt on the left and `xmark` on the right. It stays in the same sheet.
- **Pairing task:** SCAN CODE opens with a camera or a paste field. CHECK ADDRESS pins the PAIR WITH THIS MAC commit with a checkmark. CONFIRM ON MAC waits for approval and keeps × available to close. SCAN CODE and CONFIRM ON MAC have no pinned commit.

### Primary and secondary actions
- One filled action per screen, at most. On a task sheet it is the pinned commit button. On a page it is usually absent, because the page itself is the content.
- Secondary actions are full-width `GoopButton` outlines, stacked 12 apart, below the content they act on. They are never placed side by side.
- The activities card's ADD ACTIVITY and START ACTIVITY are in-card actions, not secondary outlines, and are the one pair that shares a row (The Entry-Pair Rule).
- Navigation-like actions are whole rows or cards with `chevron.right` (push or sub-step) or `chevron.down` (opens a choice sheet).
- A header right slot holds at most one `GoopHeaderButton`. `.info` draws `info.circle` 25pt; `.edit` draws `pencil` 21pt, `iconMuted`, in a 44×44 target labelled "Edit activity". `GoopDetailHeader` and `GoopWorkoutHeader` accept both. No screen uses the pencil yet.

### Destructive actions
- **What exists:** DELETE ACTIVITY, UNPAIR and DISCARD ALL use `GoopButton(role: .destructive)`, a full-width `zone5` outline, 50 tall, with `labelCard`. UNPAIR is after ABOUT at the end of More, before Debug's DEVELOPER section. DISCARD ALL is last on the Unsent page under More. Its alert counts the activities it deletes from the iPhone. Delete activity, Unpair, Replace pairing and Discard unsent activities use centred system alerts with a destructive verb and Cancel. Replace pairing names the new Mac's host and port. The D8 probe could not make the destructive verb orange while preserving its system role. The owner approved iOS's own alert colours in cleanup spec §13.5; the trigger buttons remain orange.
- **The rule:**
  - **Trigger:** a destructive action is a full-width outline button in `zone5` (stroke and label), 50 tall with radius 12. It is the **last item** on its page or sheet, alone on its row, and only for things that can be destroyed. Leave the 30pt section gap (`Goop.Metric.sectionGap`) above the button so it never touches the preceding row or card. Red (`recoveryLow`) is never used.
  - **Confirm:** a **system** dialog is always used, never a drawn one. Its title is a question naming the object ("Delete this activity?"). Its message says where the change applies (phone, Mac, Google Health). The buttons are a destructive verb and Cancel.
  - **Unpair with waiting workouts.** Use two verbs only when the current Mac has a waiting workout or a session in progress. Show “Unpair, keep workouts”, “Unpair, delete workouts” and “Cancel”. The message says the phone deletes its saved scores and gives the number of workouts still waiting for that Mac.
  - **The dialog is a centred system `.alert`** for every destructive action (owner decision 2026-11-12). Never `.confirmationDialog`.
- **No undo toast.** If an undo is ever wanted, it is a `GoopBanner` with a trailing text action in the Home banner slot, using the same slot as "Too short to save.".

### Form rows and pickers
- **What exists:**
  - **More rows:** `GoopRow` replaced the private `line()` helper. Each row is a `controlFill` slab with radius 10 and a 48 minimum height, an uppercase `labelCard` label on the left and the value on the right in `bodySmall` `textSoft`. A row can be a button, such as TEST CONNECTION or the FIXTURE MODE `Menu`.
  - **PAIR AGAIN** follows TEST CONNECTION under MAC and opens the pairing task sheet.
  - **UNSENT ACTIVITIES** follows PAIR AGAIN under MAC when the iPhone holds activities for another or unknown Mac. It shows their count and a push chevron that opens Unsent.
  - **`GoopSheetRow`:** an unfilled key–value row inside info sheets.
  - **`GoopMetricPicker`:** a `controlFill` radius-10 label with `chevron.down`. It opens a `GoopSheet` titled `METRIC` that groups `GoopChoiceRow` rows under `GoopSectionLabel` headings. Tapping a row applies it and closes the sheet.
  - **`GoopRangeSelector`:** exclusive options, 2–4 of them.
  - **Task controls:** `GoopSearchField` owns the search field, `GoopSectionLabel` owns its group headings. `GoopChoiceRow` is a 52pt single-select row with a symbol, an uppercase name and a trailing note. The selected row has a 1.5pt `strain` stroke, a 13% `strain` fill and `checkmark.circle.fill`. Unselected rows have a `contributorBorder` hairline. A choice between numbers, such as the effort rating, puts the number in the symbol slot in Sofia `valueRow`, `textSoft`, or `strainLight` when selected, with the number's word as the title.
  - **`GoopStageRow`:** a selection disc that drives chart highlighting.
- **Time input:** `GoopTimeRow` places a system compact `DatePicker(.hourAndMinute)` inside `GoopRow`, tinted `strain` and using the supplied date range. When a window's start and end fall on different dates, each row shows its own date (`Thu 12 Mar`, `bodySmall` `textSoft`) 8pt before the time.
- **Notes:** `GoopNotesField` is the approved multi-line input for activity edits. It uses a `controlFill` slab, radius 10, minimum height 50, 15×12 padding, `bodyMid` text and the `strain` cursor. Its placeholder is "Add a note" in `text3`. Input stops at the Mac's `notes_max_chars` and is trimmed on save; an empty result means no note. At the limit, `Notes can hold up to <n> characters.` appears 8pt below in `bodySmall` `text2`.
- **Not in the system:** toggles, steppers and sliders.
- **Rules:**
  - A field in a form is a **More-style row**. The former `line()` became shared `GoopRow(_, value:, disclosure:)`, with an optional trailing `chevron.right` (12pt `textSoft`) when tapping opens a choice.
  - **No system `Menu` on product screens.** Its system font, tint and glass panel do not match ghoops, and the owner rejected it on 2026-11-14. Any choice list, short or long, uses `GoopChoiceRow` rows. Inside a task, the list is a sub-step with a `USE` commit, as for sport and effort. Outside a task, it is a `GoopSheet` where tapping a row applies it, as for the Trends metric. Only the DEBUG fixture menu keeps a `Menu`.
  - A long or searchable list uses ActivityPicker selection rows, shown inside the same task sheet.
  - Two to four exclusive options use `GoopRangeSelector`.
  - **Times:** use `GoopTimeRow`, which holds a compact system `DatePicker(.hourAndMinute)` tinted `strain`. Use this, or the chart handles described under Charts with selection, rather than drawn ± steppers.
  - **Toggles:** settings live on the Mac, so the phone has no preference switches. If one becomes unavoidable, it is a `GoopRow` holding a system `Toggle` tinted `strain` on the More tab, never inside a task.

### Section labels

Two roles have separate treatments. Above rows and controls, `GoopSectionLabel` uses `labelCard` in `iconMuted`, as in More and ActivityPicker. Above prose in an info sheet, `GoopSheetSection` uses white `labelCard`, as in the approved Recovery, Strain and Sleep sheets. Both are accessibility headings. The former ad-hoc 11.5pt treatment is gone.

### Inline banners

`GoopBanner(tone:)` has five tones: `.neutral`, `.strain`, `.positive`, `.caution` and `.muted`. All can show an optional `labelStatus` title, a trailing `GoopBannerAction` in white `labelCard`, a dismiss ×, and accessory content below. Accessory content can hold the heart-rate Settings link or device choices.

- **Neutral.** The `tile` gradient fills the banner, with `radiusBanner` (10), 12pt vertical and 14pt horizontal padding. A 15pt light symbol uses `symbolColor`, defaulting to `caution`. The text is `bodyMid` in `legendText`. Home banners stack 8pt apart under the header. An old pairing link shows a neutral info banner with a dismiss button.
- **Tinted:** base colour at 12% fill, a 1pt stroke at 25%, `radiusBannerTinted` (12), 11pt vertical and 13pt horizontal padding. The symbol is 18pt semibold, the title `labelStatus`, and text `bodySmall` in `textSoft`. `.strain` uses `strainLight` ink over `strain`; `.positive` and `.caution` use their named colours; `.muted` uses `text2`.
- **Wrappers:** `HeartRateBanner` maps connection states onto these tones. The workout save banner uses `.strain`. Both retain their identifiers.
- **Placement:** banners are inline and never float. There are no toasts, snackbars or popovers.
- Saved-file permission errors never show iOS's text. Wait silently while protected data is unavailable, then retry. An unlocked read failure says "ghoops couldn't read its saved activities on this phone. It will try again." or "ghoops couldn't read your waiting changes on this phone. It will try again." A refused START shows the activity sentence inside its sheet; ADD keeps the waiting-changes sentence inside its sheet. Clear the error when the operation recovers.

### Empty, loading and pending states
- **Loading:** `GoopLoading`, a `ProgressView` tinted `text2` over "Loading from your Mac" in `bodySmall`, centred in the area that is loading. Cards keep their frame.
- **Empty or missing within a card:** `GoopPlaceholder`, one `bodyMid` `text2` sentence from spec §14.3 (for example "No heart rate recorded for this workout"). In list cards it is left-aligned ("No activities yet").
- **Whole screen:** `GoopFullState`, which shows `GoopMark` 56pt at 40% opacity, a `heading` title, `bodyMid` `text2` text (300 wide at most) and an optional 240-wide outline button.
- **Null value:** `—` in `text2`. A null ring shows the track only.
- **Stale:** values at 55% opacity plus a `clock` banner.
- **Pending (waiting on the Mac or band):**
  - In a row, the trailing `SAVING` label in `text2`, or `WAITING FOR MAC` in `caution`, set with `labelMicro`.
  - While saving, the workout subtitle uses the suffix ` · Saving to your Mac` in its existing style.
  - A dependent value the phone cannot compute shows `—` in `text2` with `UPDATING` in `labelMicro` in the comparison slot. This holds wherever the value appears: dials, rows, Home tiles, info-sheet totals and scales, and spoken labels. An insight about a pending score says it will update once the Mac has the change, keeps only what the phone computed (such as the new hours), and never repeats the old band (issue #32).
  - `SAVING` and `UPDATING` wait 0.6 s, then stay visible for at least 0.5 s. Waiting and refusal states appear at once.
  - A refusal shows a caution banner titled `NOT SAVED`, naming the activity and saying what was put back. Home shows up to three, newest first.
  - Home shows a neutral waiting banner below Undo and refusal banners, with the sync glyph in `caution` and a sentence giving the number of waiting changes.
  - On a page, the tinted strain banner whose text says what will happen and when.
  - Calibration: the caption "Calibrating" or "Calibrating: 3 of 7 nights" plus the calibration bar.
- Every one of these states is a sentence saying why. A state never shows only a spinner or only an icon.

### Charts with selection
- **What exists:**
  - `Canvas` charts drawn with `GoopPlot`.
  - Grid lines are 1pt `plotGrid` or `chartGrid`. Axis numbers are Sofia 11 in `iconMuted`, `textSoft` or `tickLabel`. Day labels are Nunito, with the current day in white at 700.
  - Start and end times use `axisTime` white at the ends.
  - Window edges are dashed white verticals with baseline dots.
  - Areas use `Gradient.hrArea` or `workoutArea`, and a null day is a gap.
  - The current day gets a white column at 5–7%.
  - Every chart has an `accessibilityChartDescriptor`.
- **Selection today is off-chart:** tapping a `GoopStageRow` under the hypnogram dims other stages to 25% (awake to 17.5%). Week charts label every point. There is no scrubbing, dragging or tooltip.
- **Minimal extension for picking a time window (provisional):**
  - The existing dashed start and end markers **become the handles**. They get a 44pt-wide invisible hit area and a selection haptic on snap, with no visible knob, grip or time bubble.
  - The in-window run uses `workoutLine` + `workoutArea`, and the run outside the window uses `neutral` at 50%.
  - The two times show under the markers in `axisTime` and in `GoopRow`s below the chart card.
  - The original window, if shown at all, uses the typical-range idiom (`typicalFill` band with `typicalEdge` dashes), not a new dashed box.
  - Results that change appear in the existing comparison idiom: a value plus a triangle plus the prior value beneath in `compareRow` `text2` (`GoopValue`), or a `GoopChip` delta. The values are never struck through.
  - On the sleep chart (`GoopSleepWindowChart`), stage blocks and the heart-rate line outside the window use `neutral` at 50%, and Google's window is a typical-range strip under the axis.

**The Mac's-Limits Rule.** A task sheet offers only what the Mac accepts. Handles, pickers, merge rows and day choices stop at `status.edit_limits` without words: the graph ends where the limit is. Add a sentence only where a control stops and nothing on screen says why, as for a full notes field and a missing pencil (edit-limits-spec.md §5.4).

### Signature: ScoreRing and the notched card
`ScoreRing` is a butt-capped stroke on a `ringTrack` track. It animates in over 0.6s ease-out only when Reduce Motion is off, and the fixture and widget variants skip the animation. Detail dials put the gh○●ps wordmark (`wordmarkDial`, size 24) above the score. The notched contributor card sits 26pt under the detail dial with its notch pointing up at the dial, and is the visual link between a score and its causes.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** apply a change on the phone first and say in words when it is still syncing.
- **Do** take every colour, gradient and radius from `Goop.Color`, `Goop.Gradient` and `Goop.Metric`. Add a token before using a new value.
- **Do** set type with `.goop(.style)`, using Sofia for numbers and Nunito for words, uppercase tracked for labels, sentence case for sentences.
- **Do** make the whole row or card the button, with `chevron.right` 12pt `textSoft` when it pushes.
- **Do** build a new task as a task sheet: `xmark` on the right, a centred `labelNav` title, rows in the middle, one `strain` commit button pinned at the bottom.
- **Do** build a new explanation as a `GoopSheet` info sheet with `GoopSheetSection` and `GoopSheetRow`, and show its numbers as rows, breakdowns and scales (The Numbers-In-Rows Rule).
- **Do** stack secondary actions as full-width outline buttons, 12 apart, below the content.
- **Do** put a destructive action last, as a full-width `zone5` outline, confirmed by a centred system `.alert` that says where the change applies.
- **Do** show pending and stale states in words: a tinted banner on the page and `WAITING FOR MAC` on the row.
- **Do** show change as value, triangle and prior value (`GoopValue`) or as a `GoopChip` delta.
- **Do** check every new component in `ComponentGallery` at the default size and at accessibility 5, and keep 44pt targets.

### Don't:
- **Don't** show `NO HR` for a missing zone or Strain, or show a lost reading at full strength (The Live Reading Rule).
- **Don't** let a number look surer than it is. A number the phone estimates says so where it is shown, not only in an info sheet. A score whose scale is still provisional says so in its info sheet and in any sentence that advises on it, such as the Strain Target. A value that comes from the band's or Fitbit's own estimate (sleep stages, HRV, SpO2) is never called a measurement (The Honest Number Rule, `docs/goop/data-honesty-audit.md`).
- **Don't** give one sport two names. A workout whose type is in the ghoops sport list carries that list's name (`Walking`, not the watch's `Walk`) on every screen, row, sheet, widget and Live Activity, before and after the Mac answers. The Mac sets the name, and the phone's instant-save overlay uses the same list, so the name never changes when a save settles (The One Name Rule, issue #16).
- **Don't** show the system's error text. Every error a person sees is a ghoops sentence that says what failed and what happens next (`ghoops couldn't read its saved activities on this phone. It will try again.`), never iOS's own message. Use Core's `ErrorCopy.message` with the operation's context. A request timeout says the Mac is taking too long; only a failed connection says the Mac can't be reached. An error the app recovers from by itself, such as a file it can't read while the phone is locked, shows nothing. An error banner clears once the thing it reports works again (The Own Words Rule, issues #23 and #24). The goop lint's `rawerror` rule rejects `localizedDescription` outside Debug files.
- **Don't** dim a value the phone computed exactly.
- **Don't** use capsule or pill buttons. Capsules belong only to the date control.
- **Don't** put text buttons (Cancel, Save, Back, Done, Edit) in a header. Headers hold icon buttons only.
- **Don't** draw iOS controls (switches, steppers, action sheets, alerts). Use the system control or the existing Goop component.
- **Don't** place two buttons side by side, except the activities card's entry pair (The Entry-Pair Rule), or put a button inside a row that is itself a button.
- **Don't** use red (`recoveryLow`) or a hand-picked pink for delete. Destructive is `zone5`.
- **Don't** strike through old values to show an edit.
- **Don't** write a calculation, a threshold list or a pair of times as a sentence in an explanation sheet. Use a breakdown, a scale or a window bar.
- **Don't** put a word, unit or letter in a Sofia string. Write ranges as numbers and set units in Nunito beside the number (The Two Voices Rule).
- **Don't** type a score-engine number, such as a band edge, a zone edge, a window or a minimum count, into a label, footnote or sentence. Build it from the Mac's value, and leave it out when the Mac sends none ([engine-numbers-spec.md](docs/goop/engine-numbers-spec.md)).
- **Don't** copy one of the Mac's request limits (edit horizon, shift, merge gap or count, notes length, activity length, heart-rate span) into the app. Read `status.edit_limits`, and drop the bound when the Mac sends none.
- **Don't** add toasts, snackbars, floating panels or drop shadows.
- **Don't** add CUSTOMIZE, a floating +, coach, Plan, Community or a strain target to Home (spec §8.1; `GHealthUITests.swift:16` asserts CUSTOMIZE is absent).
- **Don't** add new light-blue tints (`#4FB6F5`, `#7FD0FF`, `#0B3550` as literals). Use `strain` or a named token.
- **Don't** use a dashed stroke for anything except detected effort, the typical range, nap credit or window edges.
- **Don't** add settings switches on the phone. Settings live on the Mac (`ghoops config set`).
- **Don't** use superseded edit mockups. The current `docs/goop/mockups/edit.html` and its `edit-*.png` renders were approved on 2026-11-12; use them with `edit-spec.md`.
